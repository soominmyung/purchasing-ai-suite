F3. OilRing — demand has been steady over the past year. [3]

The historical customs hold is not added because the supplier reports changing its paperwork checks and no continuing delay is documented. [F1, F2]
2. Lead time, BushingSleeve, GasketRing, ValveCap, SealPlate, OilRing: ==18 → 22 weeks==: The supplier's announced extension applies to all products, and each item's revised order date falls within the stated refit period. No item-specific exception is documented. The previous customs hold is not treated as an additional ongoing delay. [F1, F2]
